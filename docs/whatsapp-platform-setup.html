01The big idea

We run one Meta app — Ideal Intelligence — for every client. We never create a new app per business. Each client connects their own WhatsApp number from inside the Tawasul dashboard (Settings → Connect WhatsApp). From then on their customers' messages flow to Tawasul, which categorizes them with AI and replies on the business's behalf.

04How Embedded Signup works

  1. CLIENT
    Owner logs into Tawasul → Settings → WhatsApp Connection → Connect WhatsApp.
  2. SYSTEM
    The Facebook JS SDK opens Meta's Embedded Signup popup.
  3. CLIENT
    Logs in with their Facebook business account, selects/creates a WhatsApp Business Account, verifies the number by SMS/voice.
  4. SYSTEM
    The browser receives a short‑lived code; the popup posts back waba_id + phone_number_id.
  5. SYSTEM
    The page calls /api/whatsapp/connect.
  6. SYSTEM
    Tawasul (using the App Secret) swaps the code for the business's long‑lived token, subscribes our app to their WABA, and upserts their whatsapp_accounts row to connected.
🔐 The App Secret is used only on Tawasul's server. Access tokens are never sent to the browser.

07A‑to‑Z: connecting a NEW business

What the client needs first

  • A phone number not active on the WhatsApp app or WhatsApp Business app (a number lives on the app or the API, not both). Fresh number is easiest.
  • A Facebook account that controls (or can create) their business.
  • Ability to receive an SMS / voice code on that number.

The steps

  1. YOU
    Provision the business in Tawasul (create the business + an owner login).
  2. CLIENT
    Logs into tawasul.idealailabs.com → Settings → WhatsApp Connection.
  3. CLIENT
    Clicks Connect WhatsApp → logs in with their Facebook business account.
  4. CLIENT
    Selects/creates their WhatsApp Business Account and verifies their number (SMS/voice).
  5. SYSTEM
    Tawasul exchanges the token, subscribes our app to their WABA, writes their whatsapp_accounts row → connected; Settings flips to "Connected".
  6. CLIENT
    Adds a payment method to their WhatsApp account (Model A), or you handle billing (Model B).
  7. YOU
    Set the per‑business AI mode (Auto‑reply / Suggest / Off) and tune its knowledge.
  8. SYSTEM
    A customer messages the number → Tawasul webhook → routed by phone_number_id → AI categorizes + replies → a card appears in the business's inbox ✅

08Billing models

Model A — client pays Meta

The client's card is on their WABA. Meta bills them directly. You invoice your service fee separately.

Best for: clean separation, zero billing risk.

Model B — you pay Meta

Your card is on the WABA. Meta bills you; you send one all‑in invoice (your fee + messaging, marked up).

Best for: "I handle everything, one bill" simplicity.

09Testing

Meta Test Number (WhatsApp → API Setup) — free for 90 days, no payment method. Or the Tawasul Simulator in the inbox — exercise categorization + AI replies with no real WhatsApp traffic.
