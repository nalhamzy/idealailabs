01The big idea

We run one Meta app — Ideal Intelligence — for every client. We never create a new app per business. Each client connects their own WhatsApp number to our app through a self‑serve page on idealailabs.com. From then on, their customers' messages flow to our server, which replies on their behalf.

04How Embedded Signup works

  1. CLIENT
    Opens idealailabs.com/onboard and clicks Connect WhatsApp.
  2. SYSTEM
    The Facebook JS SDK opens Meta's Embedded Signup popup.
  3. CLIENT
    Logs in with their Facebook business account, selects/creates a WhatsApp Business Account, verifies the number by SMS/voice.
  4. SYSTEM
    The browser receives a short‑lived code; the popup posts back waba_id + phone_number_id.
  5. SYSTEM
    The page sends these to /api/whatsapp/onboard.
  6. SYSTEM
    The server (using the secret App Secret) swaps the code for the client's long‑lived token, subscribes our app to their WABA, stores the connection, and emails the team.
🔐 The App Secret is used only on the server. Access tokens are never sent to the browser.

06One‑time platform setup

  1. DONE
    Business verification ✅ and Embedded Signup config "ES Config" (2190411671691417) ✅
  2. YOU
    Set all env vars in .env.local and Vercel.
  3. YOU
    Deploy the site: vercel --prod
  4. YOU
    Run the DB migration in production: npm run db:migrate
  5. YOU
    Webhook → Meta → WhatsApp → Configuration: Callback URL https://idealailabs.com/api/whatsapp/webhook, Verify token = WHATSAPP_VERIFY_TOKEN → Verify and save → subscribe messages.
  6. YOU
    Allowlist idealailabs.com in Meta → App settings → Facebook Login for Business.
  7. YOU
    For unlimited clients: complete App Review + Access Verification (Tech Provider). Until then you can onboard a limited number for testing.

07A‑to‑Z: connecting a NEW business

What the client needs first

  • A phone number not currently active on the WhatsApp app or WhatsApp Business app (a number lives on the app or the API, not both). Fresh number is easiest.
  • A Facebook account that controls (or can create) their business.
  • Ability to receive an SMS / voice code on that number.

The steps

  1. YOU
    Send the client their onboarding link: https://idealailabs.com/onboard
  2. CLIENT
    Opens the link, clicks Connect WhatsApp.
  3. CLIENT
    Logs in with their Facebook business account.
  4. CLIENT
    Selects or creates their Meta Business portfolio + WhatsApp Business Account.
  5. CLIENT
    Enters their business phone number and verifies it with the SMS/voice code.
  6. SYSTEM
    Embedded Signup finishes → our server exchanges the token, subscribes our app to their WABA, stores the connection, and emails you "New WhatsApp client connected".
  7. CLIENT
    Adds a payment method to their WhatsApp account (Model A) — or you handle billing (Model B).
  8. YOU
    (Optional) Tailor the bot for this client — business name, tone, FAQ / knowledge.
  9. SYSTEM
    A customer messages the client's number → the webhook receives it → routes it to this client → the bot replies ✅
When a message arrives, the webhook verifies the signature, finds the matching connection by phone_number_id, marks the message read, generates a reply, and sends it using that client's token.

08Billing models

Model A — client pays Meta

The client's card is on their WABA. Meta bills them directly for conversations. You invoice your service fee separately.

Best for: clean separation, zero billing risk.

Model B — you pay Meta

Your card is on the WABA. Meta bills you; you send one all‑in invoice (your fee + messaging, marked up).

Best for: "I handle everything, one bill" simplicity.

Meta only governs who pays for messages. Your setup/management/service fee is your own arrangement.

09Testing without a real client

Use Meta's free Test Number (WhatsApp → API Setup panel). Free messages for 90 days, no payment method, no risk to real client accounts — ideal for confirming the send / receive / reply loop.
